## Beyond the requests

- Settings pages say only what exists: the Downloads page reports this profile's ready downloads and their size, explains Play / Show in folder / Delete, and links to the P2P engine for the separate torrent cache. Downloads keep their private, owned storage; no destination picker is offered because downloads are deliberately not written to user folders (CLAUDE.md, OfflineLibrary).
- Light mode is a palette system, not a single theme: every preset has a light form, and the gallery previews presets as they will be drawn.
- Row arrows follow one rule across the app instead of a per-component choice, and the existing "Row arrows" setting now governs every row.

## Privacy

`PRIVACY.md` and `PRIVACY.ar.md` (version 1.0, effective 2026-11-01) come from reading the code paths, not assumptions:

- Local state: `safeStorage`/DPAPI on Windows, AES-256-GCM under a non-extractable WebCrypto key on the web, no plaintext fallback (`electron/main.mjs`, `web/store.js`).
- Account service: `server/accounts/worker.mjs` and `migrations/0001_accounts.sql` store username, salted+peppered proof hash, wrapped keys, ciphertext, size, revision and hashed session tokens; IPs only as peppered hashes in attempt counters; bodies never logged; `DELETE /v1/account` deletes chunks, sessions and the user row; sessions end after 180 days unused (docs/ACCOUNTS.md).
- Usage statistics: `core/usage.mjs` sends `{token, platform, version}` only after opt-in, every two minutes while active; the Worker stores a peppered token hash, platform, version and last-seen time; records older than 31 days are deleted by a daily cron; opt-out sends a delete that revokes the record. The web smoke exercises opt-in, account sync isolation and opt-out against a local Worker.
- Defaults that contact third parties without a link: installed addons (Cinemeta by default), metahub artwork, Wikidata/Commons/OpenStreetMap credits and maps, iTunes/Deezer theme songs (`themeSong: auto`), GitHub update checks and the notice feed. Opt-in: tracker history and scrobbling, skip segments, Discord presence, webhooks, AI search, usage statistics.
- Torrents expose the IP address to peers, trackers and the DHT; UPnP/NAT-PMP follow `torrentIncoming` (on by default); TorrServer contacts its own services.

Open points for the owner (not guessed in the policy):
1. The operator is named only as the owner of the GitHub account ABADIOSA. A legal name, country and any registration are the owner's to add.
2. There is no dedicated privacy contact; the policy uses GitHub issues and asks people to keep secrets out of them. A private address (or GitHub's private vulnerability reporting) would be better.
3. Cloudflare and GitHub retain request data under their own terms; the policy links their policies and makes no promise for them.
4. The policy claims no compliance with PDPL, GDPR or any other law. A legal review is needed before any such claim.
5. The in-app links point to `blob/main/PRIVACY.md`; they resolve once this branch is merged.
