## The wheel (owner's request)

Executive interpretation (chosen here, not attributed to the owner): a wheel whose hub lies just beyond the window's side edge — right in Arabic, left in English — with the destinations on its rim. The visible part is the arc nearest the content.

- **Turning.** Choosing a destination turns the rim's ticks and a glass lens about the hub to it (300 ms, `--ease-turn`). The destinations themselves do not move: a target that slid after a press would let a quick second click land on another page, and every destination must stay one click away without turning anything. Text and icons stay upright.
- **Sizes.** Compact (default): a 92 px rail, 44 px round targets, steps of 48–62 px, the open page named above the wheel, names as glass tooltips on hover/focus. Open: 236 px, each destination's whole line (icon and name) is its target. The arc bulges 22 px (compact) or 34 px (open) past its ends. A window too short for the minimum step lets the wheel scroll on its own.
- **Order.** Home, Discover, My library, Collections, Downloads, Addons, Music, Live TV, Settings on the rim; profile, Stremio link and the open/compact toggle below. Hidden destinations, locks, quiet interface and the update notice behave as before.
- **Keyboard and semantics.** A `<nav>` of buttons in reading order with `aria-current="page"`; Tab walks through them; Arrow Up/Down move to the neighbour, Home/End to the first/last; focus rings are visible. No wheel-event capture.
- **Narrow windows.** Below 761 px the drawer holds the open wheel; the content takes the full width.
- **Migration.** `navStyle` and `glass` are appearance settings validated by `safeAppearance` (backups, accounts, shared design codes carry them). Settings saved before the wheel get them on load (`adoptWheel` via `retireLineless`) with everything else kept; a viewer can choose the old sidebar. `wheelOpen` is a validated setting. Limit: a 0.54 device syncing through a Riwaq account does not know `navStyle`; if it rewrites the appearance, a viewer's "sidebar" choice can come back as the wheel on the newer device (the default), never the other way round. Nothing else in the appearance is affected.
- **Rollback.** Appearance → Interface → Navigation → Sidebar brings back the 0.54 layout per profile; Buttons and dialogs → Solid removes the glass. Reverting the branch leaves `navStyle`, `glass` and `wheelOpen` in saved settings, which 0.54 ignores.

## Glass and motion

Inspired by [Apple's Materials guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) and the [Liquid Glass overview](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/TechnologyOverviews/liquid-glass); drawn with CSS for Electron and the web. It is not Apple's system material.

- Palette-tinted translucent fill, a top sheen, a thin luminous edge and a short shadow for primary, secondary, destructive, choice and icon buttons; pressed sinks (scale 0.97); disabled flat; one focus ring.
- Backdrop blur only on floating surfaces (dialogs, menus, wheel tooltips) — never per card or per button.
- Opaque under Appearance → Interface → Buttons and dialogs: Solid, under `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `forced-colors`, or without `backdrop-filter`. Over pictures (showcase, theater) the glass stays dark in both schemes.
- Motion tokens: press 150 ms, pages 220 ms (fade; page bodies also rise 6 px), wheel 300 ms; 0 with Reduce motion or `prefers-reduced-motion`. Only transform and opacity animate; nothing waits for an animation.
- HUD: a blur in the HUD's window has no video under it to sample (MPV is a native surface), so the bar keeps its dark layer with the same edge and sheen.

Measured contrast from screenshots (pixels behind the text, not tokens): primary button 10.1:1 dark, 4.9:1 light; the open wheel's current name 16.0:1 dark, 17.8:1 light (`.cache/ui-feedback/contrast-*.json`). Arbitrary custom palettes and wallpapers are not certified.
